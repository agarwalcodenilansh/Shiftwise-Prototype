Implementation Roadmap: Swap Rules Layer

Iterative 6-sprint rollout architecture to de-risk delivery.

Sprint 1 Compliance Gate
Certificate Block

Hard validation layer against credential registry. Blocks assignments with expired certificates prior to scheduling, eliminating compliance risk for CarePoint.

Sprints 2–3 Core Engine
Swap Rules & Auto-Approval

Pure functional rules engine, role training validation, customer policy toggles, and instant auto-approval for qualified low-risk shift exchanges.

Sprint 4 Cost Guardrail
Overtime as a Rule

Weekly hours aggregator with configurable 48h limit. Configurable over-limit actions: routing to location manager (FreshBowl) or regional escalation (UrbanMart).

Sprints 5–6 Pilot & Rollout
Rollout & Buffer

Controlled multi-tenant rollout with FreshBowl, CarePoint, and UrbanMart. Real-time exception telemetry, override dashboards, and deployment buffer.
